# Abu Dhabi Real Estate Brokerage Feasibility Study

Premium decision dashboard evaluating the commercial, regulatory, operational and financial feasibility of launching a real-estate brokerage in Abu Dhabi.

Prepared by **Elissa Fahd**. Research snapshot: **21 August 2026**.

The financial model contains planning assumptions and is not legal, tax or investment advice. Statutory requirements, licensing costs and supplier pricing should be revalidated before capital commitment.
